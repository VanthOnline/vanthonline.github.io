The Saga of Vanth — A Development Chronicle

Vanth Online is a custom fantasy MMORPG based on The Saga of Vanth book series. Players create a character and explore a hand-built world — beginning with a boat arrival at the coastal settlement of Wreckwater Reach, where a guided tutorial teaches movement, combat, equipment and survival before players strike out toward the town of Blackport.

The Saga of Vanth is the culmination of decades of tabletop adventuring refined into a series of novels which are the basis for the lore of Vanth Online. We hope to preserve some of the spirit that birthed this world in the interpretation of it as an online game.

There is still considerable work to be done bringing this to life, but always at the very end of the checklist is... have fun.

How to read the labels: every entry carries a colored stamp — Verified in-game means it was played and confirmed working; Built – needs testing means it is running on the server but still needs hands-on testing; Designed / planned means agreed on paper but not yet in the game (including staged proposals); Investigating means still being worked out.

Where things stand today

  • The tutorial & the boat ride are complete and confirmed working in the game.
  • Coins and one Saltworn Band have reached players in controlled game tests. The Band appeared with a loot notice and a completed reward record.
  • The armor collection and Wreckwater reward rules are built. Other item types, ordinary reward chances and recovery still need game tests.
  • A new beach wilderness is staged for owner review, with protected arrival spaces. No new permanent creatures have been deployed.
  • The next reward session will test silver chunks, the Blood Sucker prize, duplicate claims, full packs and reconnect recovery.
  • Undead ambushes and later adventures remain planned for future development.

Nov 19, 2026

A new Wreckwater wilderness takes its first steps

An AI-assisted population plan moved from terrain analysis into the live world: forty new spawn locations replaced much of the inherited beach wildlife. The first in-game walkthrough confirmed a believable spread of creatures, preserved special encounters, and a quieter, more deliberate opening coast. This is a working proof of description-led world building in one region, not yet a finished or automated system for the whole game.

A living population, with room to improve

The beach now reliably offers two Greedy Treasure Hunters and one Shipwrecked Madman alongside its wildlife; both Hunters were found during an in-game check. Mosquito and scavenger movement changes were installed, and an inaccessible Madman was relocated. His long-distance movement and final appearance still need verification, so the beach remains a work in progress.

Reliable item delivery proves its value

Controlled in-game tests delivered both a Saltworn Band and a Saltworn Chunk of Silver directly into inventory, with visible reward notices and matching completed claim records. The significance is larger than either item: Vanth can award a specific player an item and account for the delivery without relying on a corpse. Natural drop chances, special rewards, and further recovery cases still need playtesting.

Nov 15–17, 2026

Building the playable world comes first

Development committed to the current EverQuest-based engine and a practical sequence: finish Wreckwater Beach, build its tunnel, then play a new character from level one toward levels eight to ten before expanding instanced adventures. The focus is demonstrable fun and progress rather than chasing another technology platform.

Nov 14–17, 2026

A full pack no longer has to mean lost loot

Supervised tests demonstrated that item delivery can recognize a full inventory, keep a pending claim, and complete the award when space becomes available. The game also showed its direct-to-inventory loot notification. This establishes a recovery path for rewards rather than silently discarding them, although additional persistence and edge-case tests remain.

Nov 13–14, 2026

Every eligible adventurer earns their own coin

In-game kills confirmed automatic currency rewards scaled to the defeated creature, not the player. Eligible nearby group members each receive the full personal payout. It is an early building block for Vanth’s aim of supporting both solo play and cooperative adventures without penalizing either.

House Rules & Design Bibles

Permanent Operating Rules

Nothing touches the live game without a backup and explicit approval; investigation is read-only by default. Major work runs a formal review first — goal, success measures, alternatives and risks — with the owner making the call. Also covers solo-friendly progression and dialogue-writing standards.

Loot System Design

The approved model: rewards are decided per fight and appear directly in your pack — there is no corpse-looting in Vanth. Coins are automatic; equipment is personalized to the character.

Corpse Surprise Design

Approved design, not yet built: sometimes the slain rise again as a surprise undead ambush — entirely separate from loot, in three tiers of danger (Ordinary, Deathbound, Dread).

Reward Overflow Design

The approved design calls for rewards to wait when a pack is full. The current delivery candidate still needs its full-pack and reconnect tests; exceptional losses before a claim is recorded may be handled by the GM.

Progression & Adventure Design

Playable solo from start to finish: meaningful rewards for time invested rather than fast leveling, adaptive challenges only in special instanced adventures, and no mandatory group roles.

Content Registry

Every custom asset carries a stable identity, build notes and provenance, so content can be tracked, rebuilt or moved later without losing its history.
