About the Project

Vanth Online is a custom fantasy MMORPG based on The Saga of Vanth book series. Players create a character and explore a hand-built world — beginning with a boat arrival at the coastal settlement of Wreckwater Reach, where a guided tutorial teaches movement, combat, equipment and survival before players strike out toward the town of Blackport.

Development happens on a private development server, in regular working sessions. This page is the running record of that work — the newest progress at the top, older milestones below.

How to read the labels: every entry carries a colored stamp — Verified in-game means it was played and confirmed working; Built – needs testing means it is running on the server but still needs hands-on testing; Designed / planned means agreed on paper but not yet built; Investigating means still being worked out.

Where things stand today

  • The tutorial & the boat ride are complete and confirmed working in the game.
  • The Wreckwater reward system is installed and running — a full hands-on playtest is still to be scheduled.
  • The armor collection (480 pieces) is built and ready; trading between players not yet tried.
  • Coin rewards & safe item delivery are tested and working in-game.
  • Zone map work is being investigated, step by step.
  • Undead ambushes & full-pack safeguards are designed but not yet built.

Nov 17–18, 2026

Old editor tool retired

An unfinished in-house editor from early development has been removed from the game server. Checks confirmed no leftover editor-made content remains and every originally placed item is untouched. The game behaves exactly as before; one routine restart will finish clearing it out.

Full Wreckwater reward system goes live

The beach's complete reward system is now running on the game server: beach kills have a 25% chance to award a ring (the Saltworn Band the first time, then sellable Saltworn Silver chunks after that); the Blood Sucker boss always awards a reusable self-healing potion; any kill still has a 10% chance to award a piece of armor picked to fit your character; and coins continue automatically as before. Rewards appear straight in your pack — there are no lootable corpses — and each fight produces a single combined "You have new loot!" notice. Still needed: a full hands-on playtest in the game (14 checks).

Nov 16–17, 2026

Big-picture decisions for the project

Three owner decisions shaped the road ahead. The project stays on its current development platform (a move to a different game engine was considered and set aside). A prototype that tried to play the game automatically was retired — real playtesting turned out to be faster. And custom potion-selling was ruled out of scope. A formal "think before building" review is now required for all major work.

House Rules & Design Bibles

Permanent Operating Rules

Nothing touches the live game without a backup and explicit approval; investigation is read-only by default. Major work runs a formal review first — goal, success measures, alternatives and risks — with the owner making the call. Also covers solo-friendly progression and dialogue-writing standards.

Loot System Design

The approved model: rewards are decided per fight and appear directly in your pack — there is no corpse-looting in Vanth. Coins are automatic; equipment is personalized to the character.

Corpse Surprise Design

Approved design, not yet built: sometimes the slain rise again as a surprise undead ambush — entirely separate from loot, in three tiers of danger (Ordinary, Deathbound, Dread).

Reward Overflow Design

An approved promise: no earned reward is ever lost, even when your pack is full. The core safety net is already proven in-game; the final layer still needs building.

Progression & Adventure Design

Playable solo from start to finish: meaningful rewards for time invested rather than fast leveling, adaptive challenges only in special instanced adventures, and no mandatory group roles.

Content Registry

Every custom asset carries a stable identity, build notes and provenance, so content can be tracked, rebuilt or moved later without losing its history.
